Goals Support

Answers for local Goals, Vision backups, optional Plus subscriptions and safe handling of earlier accounts.

Where did my Goals go?

Check whether you are viewing a sample preview, a verified local guest account or a read-only original backup. These are not the same data source. Goals does not silently merge or overwrite an older account. If you used an earlier test build, keep that app and any original backups until you have verified recovery.

How do I protect my Vision photos?

Use the app's Settings → Backups & recovery flow when your local account is verified. Follow the actual in-app export steps and retain the encrypted Files backup separately from its recovery key. A selected Files location may use iCloud Drive or another service. A photo in your iOS Photos library is not the same thing as a confirmed, restorable Goals backup.

How does Goals Plus work?

Goals Plus is an optional subscription for extended 90-day Momentum history. Only a verified Apple subscription can unlock that view; your free Vision, Goals, Tasks, Habits and Review stay available. If paid options are unavailable on your device, the service may not yet be configured in your storefront.

  • Restore: Open Settings → Goals Plus → Restore purchases in builds that include StoreKit. Restoring does not charge you.
  • Cancel: Use Manage subscriptions in your Apple Account. Cancellation stops automatic renewal, subject to Apple's terms.
  • Refund: Visit Apple's Report a Problem. Do not post Apple receipts in public issues.

Earlier test builds and account recovery

Earlier Goals editions may have used a separate signed-in account or a different archive format. A native encrypted guest key is not proof of ownership of an older account. Do not delete an earlier installation or confidential backup in an attempt to force migration. Recovery should occur only after an authenticated, independently verified import or review.

Report a problem or request private help

Our public Goals issue tracker accepts non-sensitive bug reports and requests for assistance (a GitHub account may be required). Include your iOS version, app version and a short reproducible description. Never include your Goals, account ID, photos, recovery phrase, Apple receipts, email address or other private information in a public issue.

For an account-data or privacy question, use this non-sensitive request for a private support channel. Do not share confidential information until a trusted private method has been arranged.

Goals support pages describe features by current edition. They do not mean a new paid release has already launched.
