Your plans are personal

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Goals Direction is designed to let you plan privately. This page explains the behavior of the current native, local-account edition and distinguishes earlier test editions that may have used different services.

Goals information on your device

Your Goals, Tasks, recorded completions, Habits, Vision descriptions and photos you choose, Review reflections, local Talk notes and app preferences are used to display your plan. The current native protected guest account stores its saved records encrypted on your device. Device-only Keychain material protects that account; the recovery key you save is separate.

When you explicitly export a protected backup using the Files interface, you choose where that file is saved. A location you select in Files may include a third-party or cloud storage provider. Keep recovery keys safe and separate from backups. Losing access to both device and backups may mean loss of local records.

Photos, location and reminders

  • Photos: You choose images using the system photo picker for Vision content. Selecting a photo does not authorize uploading your full library to a Goals server.
  • Nearby tasks: If you deliberately use the optional Nearby function, iOS may ask for foreground location permission to compare your location with places you chose. The feature is not a continuous background location history.
  • Review reminders: If you request a reminder, iOS may ask for notification permission to schedule it on the device. Permission is not requested just to read your Goals.

Online services, AI and analytics

Cloud synchronization and live AI responses are not connected in the current native local-account edition. Local Talk notes are not live AI conversations. The current native source does not integrate an advertising or third-party behavioral tracking SDK. We do not sell your Goals, notes or Vision photos to advertisers.

This website is hosted through GitHub Pages and may generate standard access logs governed by GitHub's privacy statement. Visiting an external link uses that service's own privacy practices.

Goals Plus and payments

Optional Goals Plus subscriptions, when offered, use Apple's in-app purchase system. The app checks Apple-verified subscription entitlement to determine whether extended history is available. We do not collect or store your payment card number. Apple handles purchasing, subscription management and refunds under its own terms. Prices and availability must be shown by Apple's storefront; this website does not start a payment.

Older app versions and original account information

Some earlier Goals test builds may have used an account system or remote services distinct from the current native guest store. Opening or deleting the native edition does not necessarily delete any older account records, and there is no automatic migration of an original account. Please do not remove the older app or discard an original backup before separately confirming you have a verified recoverable copy.

Choices, retention and questions

Use the app's own saved-data and encrypted backup controls to manage local information. Removing an app may erase local-only content if a separate usable backup was not kept. Data stored with any earlier third-party service must be reviewed separately and is not erased solely by deleting the native app.

For a privacy inquiry or a request involving an older account, start a private-channel request without posting credentials, personal data, Goals, recovery keys or receipts in a public issue. We will not ask for your recovery phrase in a public forum.
